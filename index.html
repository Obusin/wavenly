01 — What we build

The inquiry path.

Pick a stage to see what usually breaks there, what we build, and what changes once it works.

Stage 01 — Found

Turn up when someone is already looking.

Search fundamentals are built into the work from the start: clean structure, pages that match the services you actually sell, and a site that performs on a phone with two bars of signal.

What breaks
One page trying to rank for six trades, no local signals, slow on mobile.
What we build
On-page SEO structure, a page per service, local relevance signals, metadata and indexing, performance essentials.
What changes
You show up for the job you want, in the area you cover.

Stage 02 — Convinced

Give people a reason to choose you.

We shape your offer, your proof, and your next step into a site that makes sense before anyone ever picks up the phone. Every page has one job.

What breaks
Stock photos, vague copy, and no answer to "why you".
What we build
Page strategy and structure, a custom visual direction, mobile-first build, licensing and credentials placed where they carry weight.
What changes
The right customer stops comparing and starts contacting.

Stage 03 — Asked

Make reaching out the obvious move.

We remove the friction between interest and contact, so a good lead never stalls on a twelve-field form or a phone number buried in a footer.

What breaks
A contact page that asks for everything and explains nothing.
What we build
Quote and booking paths, contact form design, emergency and after-hours routes, a confirmation experience that reassures.
What changes
Asking takes seconds, and people can tell what happens next.

Stage 04 — Answered

Nothing lands in an inbox nobody opens.

An inquiry is only worth what happens after it arrives. We route it to where you'll actually see it and make sure the customer hears back.

What breaks
Form mail to an address the owner stopped checking two years ago.
What we build
Lead-routing logic, notifications that reach a phone, automatic confirmations, follow-up and review requests.
What changes
Every inquiry lands somewhere a person will see it.

Stage 05 — Kept

Keep it useful when the business changes.

Your website isn't abandoned at launch. Prices move, crews grow, a service gets added. We handle the updates so the site stays true.

What breaks
A site that was accurate the week it launched and wrong ever since.
What we build
Managed updates, a private space for change requests, practical integrations, hosting and domain coordination.
What changes
The website keeps earning after the invoice is paid.
